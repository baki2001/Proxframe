# Conduct

Be direct and be kind. Disagree with the idea, not the person.

I remove comments that are abusive, that target someone, or that post other people's private details. Repeat offences lead to a block. I decide, and you can appeal by opening an issue titled "Appeal" or by writing through the security form if the matter is private.

Strong criticism of the product, including its licence, is welcome as long as it stays on the product.
