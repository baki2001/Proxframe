# Pricing

Proxframe is free for up to 3 nodes, whether that is a homelab or a company. A node is one Proxmox VE host, in a cluster or alone. The free version has every Proxmox VE and Backup Server screen and action, needs no account and has no time limit.

If Proxmox can do it, the free version can. Passkeys, second factors, step-up checks and signed updates stay free, and so do alerts to your own targets and backups to storage you already own. Licence checks run offline and nothing in the app asks you to upgrade.

Two paid things are planned and neither exists yet. A licence per node covers sites above 3 nodes and adds single sign-on, audit export, scheduled reports, a standby hub and support. A few hosted services cover what has to run outside your lab: a check that tells you when the whole lab goes quiet, phone push beyond a free monthly allowance, and an encrypted copy of your config. Beta testers get the hosted services free, and founders keep a locked price. There are no lifetime deals, because these run on my servers. Prices are set when the services ship.

On data: the warnings before risky updates download a public list and send nothing about your setup. Alerts and off-site backups go only to targets and storage you choose.
